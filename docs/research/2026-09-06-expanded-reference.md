## Source and relation decisions

- **Шапырашты, Суан:** the Auezov glossary explicitly lists both Ekey subdivisions, three Eskozha subdivisions and the three Suan labels. No inference from the personal biography section.
- **Ысты, Ошақты:** El.kz's 2021 account after the compilation by Ә. Көпіш explicitly labels internal ru/ata. Source spellings are retained, including `ІШуылдақ`; the possible reading `Шуылдақ` is only a search alias, identified as tentative.
- **Қаңлы:** two major groups, four territorial subdivisions and 23 inner labels. The same-named Baqa branches remain separate. These are territorial membership groups, not four additional tribes.
- **Арғын:** the published Bes Meiram / Zheti Momyn grouping replaces the old flat list, preserving existing IDs.
- **Керей:** the selected 2015 version places twelve groups under Abak and Shimoyyn through Siban under Ashamayly. The alternate placement of Shimoyyn is mentioned explicitly.
- **Қыпшақ:** use the four groups from the shazhere version and five internal Karakypshak groups. Do not merge Radlov's separate historical roster into this structure; Tory is not silently equated with the legacy Toqa.
- **Уақ:** the published eleven groups are retained; the last four are nested in the source's Ergenekti Uaq collective.
- **Қоңырат:** actual source is **Ж. Бейсенбайұлы, Шығармалары, III том: Зерттеулер, Астана: Өрнек, 2022, 502 pages, ISBN 978-601-7379-54-4**. It cites the separate 2016 book; the 2016 title must not be attributed to this PDF. Printed pages 465–466 and 480–491 are PDF pages +1. Kotenshi contains Bes Ata and Zhamanbai. Bes Ata has four displayed intermediate groups because Zhetimder contains Sary and Sapar. Zhamanbai includes later associated groups; its note explicitly identifies association, not descent. Baylar and Zhandar have separate child rosters. The additional collective Alty Ata overlaps those groups and is not duplicated.
- **Адай:** only the two main groups and eight arys of the published Babalar Sozi / Folkenu version are included. The following personal chains in the poem are excluded. Zhemeney follows this version; another edition's Buzau is not added as a duplicate or silently equated.
- **Таз:** the three main groups and explicit subdivisions are included; the subsequent combined list below Shoktygul and Qulai is omitted because the intermediate relation is ambiguous.
- **Беріш, Шеркеш, Табын, Тама:** source-authored lists; Tabyn's three groups are distinguished by their signs. Berish's Esen is the seventh list item across the paragraph break, not omitted.
- **Найман:** no change in this batch. The proposed personal intermediates Elata, Sugirish, Kelbuga and Ketbuga were rejected; two conflicting versions were not merged.
